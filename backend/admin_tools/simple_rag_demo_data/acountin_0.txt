DEPARTMENT!

A department of the School of Business

About Us
edit (https://en.wikiversity.org/w/index.php?title=Portal:Accounting/Intro&action=edit)

We are the Wikiversity Accounting Department, a group of people who are obsessed with
credits and debits and think accounting is fun! Our goals are to develop the world's foremost
source of free accounting knowledge, to educate all interested people about the accounting
profession, and prepare those who are interested in accountancy with the skills and
theoretical knowledge necessary to succeed. Please feel free to browse our pages, courses, and
ask questions on our talk page. We are here for you!
