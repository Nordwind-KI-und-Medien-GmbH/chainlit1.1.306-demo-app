## Summary

· Financial accounting is the act of reporting on the historical activities of an organization by
producing financial statements (defined later in this course) with accompanying notes that
allow users of those financial statements to make decisions. Users can include
management, investors, tax authorities and creditors among others.

· Managerial accounting is the process of making analyses and projections on those historical
records (along with other pieces of information) for management to make strategic decisions
regarding finance and marketing efforts.

· Cost accounting is the process of identifying the cost of inputs to producing goods and
services. This allows management to better make better supply chain management and
pricing decisions. It also allows management to determine if taking a product to market will
be viable and how much the new product/service will contribute to operating income.

. As you can see, each of these types of accounting are important for all organizations to
thrive. Without them, proper planning is not possible.
